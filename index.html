Clipper

Clipper is a small personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps him upload short video clips that he made to his own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or use it.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video clip from the owner's computer to the owner's own account.
  • On TikTok it sends the clip to the account's inbox as a draft. The owner opens the TikTok app, finishes the post and publishes it himself.
  • It uses only the official APIs of each platform, with the owner's permission (OAuth login on the platform's own page).
